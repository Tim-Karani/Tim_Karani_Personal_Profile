Chapter 01 — Identity

The person behind the work

I learned IT the practical way — in broadcast studios, server rooms and busy offices — and I bring that same practicality to every line of code I write.

I've spent nearly two years working in IT, across a TV and radio broadcaster and a busy marketing agency. I keep people, devices and business systems running, and I design and build the web applications that make everyday work easier.

At Mwangaza Inspirational Kenya Ltd, a TV and radio broadcaster, I supported IT in a production-critical environment: studios, newsroom systems and a server room that can't go offline mid-broadcast.

At Metabel Zara Ltd, a marketing agency, I've been part of the IT function since July 2025. I support office and field teams, business systems, data collection and reporting, and I document the workflows that keep it all running. Alongside that I built Faulu, a complete PHP and MySQL career-guidance platform, from the ground up.

That work rests on a KNEC Certificate (2023) and Diploma in Information Technology (2025) from Regional Center Training Institute. Every role since has brought problems nobody had written a manual for, and solving them shaped how I work: understand the problem, work through it methodically, document it, and leave things better than I found them.

Chapter 06 — Method

              
From broken to working

              
    
                
  1. 01
    Problem
    Name the actual issue — what's broken, for whom, and why it matters.
    2. 
                
  2. 02
    Investigation
    Reproduce it, ask questions, check logs, rule things out one by one.
    3. 
                
  3. 03
    Design
    Choose the simplest fix that solves it properly, not just for today.
    4. 
                
  4. 04
    Implementation
    Make the change carefully, in small steps I can undo.
    5. 
                
  5. 05
    Testing
    Confirm it works for the person who reported it — not just on my screen.
    6. 
                
  6. 06
    Improvement
    Document what happened and what would stop it happening again.

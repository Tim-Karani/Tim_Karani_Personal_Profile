My Story

My journey into technology started with curiosity — wanting to understand not just how computers work, but how they can be used to solve real problems for real people. That curiosity carried me through a Certificate and later a Diploma in Information Technology at Regional Center Training Institute, and into environments where theory had to meet practice fast.

During my industrial attachment at Mwangaza Inspirational Kenya Ltd, a media organization running TV and Radio broadcast operations, I got my first taste of structured IT support within a high-availability, production-critical environment — troubleshooting across studios, newsroom systems, and basic server room infrastructure. That experience opened up my mind to real world operations being able to use what I learnt in class and applying it to the real world. I later joined Metabel Zara Ltd, a marketing agency, where I support IT operations across office and field teams, business systems, and day-to-day technical needs.

Alongside my IT support work, I've been building my web development skills — designing and coding a full PHP-based Career Guidance System from the ground up, and more recently exploring graphic design with Photoshop. I learn best through hands-on implementation and visual workflows, and I'm open for the next practical problem to solve.

My Mission

Using technology with purpose to create solutions that serve people and make a meaningful impact.

I believe technology should make life easier, businesses stronger, and communities better. Every system I support and every line of code I write is guided by that principle — practical solutions over complexity, and real impact over showmanship.

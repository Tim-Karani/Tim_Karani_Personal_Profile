Field Operations & Retail Execution

A system concept to connect what field teams do in shops — visits, checks and photo evidence — with what supervisors and clients need to see: clear, timely reports.

Concept — not deployed or adopted by any organisation

The problem

Field marketing campaigns run across many retail outlets at once. Visit evidence, display checks and stock notes often travel through chat messages, phone galleries and spreadsheets.

That makes it hard for supervisors to know what actually happened in the field, and slow to turn the day's work into a report. Supporting field teams day to day showed me how much time this costs.

How the pieces connect

  1. 01Campaigns

    Define the campaign, outlets and what must be checked.

  2. 02Visit planning

    Assign outlets to field reps for each day.

  3. 03Check-in & evidence

    Reps complete a short checklist with photos on their phone.

  4. 04Supervisor review

    Submissions arrive in one place to approve or query.

  5. 05Reporting

    Approved visits roll up into campaign reports.

Try the core loop

Complete the four checks on the phone, then submit. The report lands in the supervisor view. Everything here is sample data running in your browser.

Design principles

Built for the field, not the office. Large touch targets, very short forms, and tolerance for weak mobile connections.

Least privilege. Reps see their own visits; supervisors see their team; only admins manage accounts.

Evidence first. A report is only as good as the proof behind it, so evidence is attached at the moment of the visit.

Private by design. No client, outlet or employee data is used in this concept — everything shown is invented.

What happens next

The next steps are to validate the workflow with the people who'd use it, produce wireframes for the rep and supervisor views, and then build a working prototype in PHP and MySQL. This page will be updated as the project moves forward.

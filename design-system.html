Design system

The rules behind the site, so it can grow without losing its identity. Everything here is defined once in assets/css/main.css.

The idea: cinematic technology

  • Film

    Title-sequence language: a dark frame, one key light, letterbox bars, slates, timecode, rack focus and a camera that moves through layers.

  • Technology

    Interface language: grids, monospace labels, real code, consoles and data flowing between systems.

  • Clarity

    Atmosphere never sits on top of reading text. Body copy is always high-contrast and still.

  • Honesty

    Every visual says what it is: real work, a working demo, an illustration or a concept.

Amber is reserved for honesty labels: it always means "illustrative, concept or sample". Teal means real, working or complete.

Typography

Archivo, a variable family with a width axis: expanded (125) for titles like a film credit, normal width for reading. JetBrains Mono for every technical label, timestamp and status.
